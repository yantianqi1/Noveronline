# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarification when needed.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Give actionable feedback with enough context for others to respond.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic disruption.
- Sharing credentials, private user content, or sensitive logs in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.

Report concerns through the security contact listed in `SECURITY.md` when private handling is needed.
